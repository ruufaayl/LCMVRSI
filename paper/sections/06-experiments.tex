\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

All experiments are deliberately small (CPU / a 4\,GB laptop GPU); the synthetic tasks isolate the
recall--memory mechanism rather than chase scale. Everything is reproducible from the repository:
a global seed is set, the software environment (library versions, git commit) is logged in every
result file, and each figure has a one-command regenerator.

\paragraph{Harness.} Models implement one \texttt{SequenceModel} interface
($\text{ids}\to\text{logits}$) and self-report \texttt{state\_size} (bytes of fixed recurrent
state, $0$ if none) and \texttt{complexity}. Benchmarks implement \texttt{generate} and
\texttt{evaluate}; recall is supervised only at query positions (loss ignores the rest). A single
runner builds model and benchmark from a declarative config, trains, evaluates held-out recall,
and writes a self-describing JSON (config, the model's complexity/state, training curve,
throughput, environment, and---for gated models---the realized write rate).

\paragraph{Models.} Six architectures at matched width/depth: a softmax-attention
\emph{transformer} (no fixed state; $O(n^2)$); \emph{linear attention}~\citep{katharopoulos2020transformers}
(fixed $O(d^2)$ state); a diagonal \emph{SSM} (S4D-style~\citep{gu2022s4}, fixed $O(dN)$);
\emph{RWKV}~\citep{peng2023rwkv} (fixed $O(d)$); \emph{Hyena}~\citep{poli2023hyena} (gated FFT long
convolution, no fixed state); and \textbf{SGSM}, the surprise-gated sparse-memory model of
Section~\ref{sec:problem} (H2), whose store is input-dependent. The subquadratic/fixed-state models
are small, honestly-named baselines, not the original authors' full kernels.

\paragraph{Tasks.} All are associative-recall probes with answers resampled per sequence (so recall
cannot be memorized in weights): \emph{MQAR}~\citep{arora2024zoology} (multi-query recall over $D$
key$\rightarrow$value pairs); \emph{copying}~\citep{jelassi2024copying}; \emph{induction} (the
induction-head rule); \emph{needle-in-a-haystack} (one binding hidden at random depth); and
\emph{structured recall}, a predictable cyclic-filler stream with $D$ scattered novel bindings
whose realized novelty is decoupled from length---the regime in which
Corollary~\ref{cor:lowentropy} permits, and SGSM is designed to exploit, sub-$n$ state.

\paragraph{Training.} AdamW with a linear-warmup/cosine-decay schedule and gradient clipping;
tiny-by-default sizes. Constant-learning-rate training left the transformer baseline undertrained
on harder instances, so the schedule and clipping are used throughout to obtain a converged
baseline before comparing against it---without it the recall--state gap would be confounded by
optimization, the very caveat raised by \citet{okpekpe2025revisiting}.

\paragraph{Metrics and studies.} We report held-out recall accuracy against (i) task difficulty
($D$), (ii) each model's fixed recurrent-state bytes and throughput (the empirical recall--memory
and recall--throughput frontiers), and (iii) for SGSM, its \emph{realized} store size (mean writes)
versus its worst case. The H2 study additionally ablates the surprise gate (forced fully open) to
isolate its effect. Results---reported with their honesty tags and including negative
outcomes---follow.
